Ahmedabad Metro System - Structured Knowledge Base

=== SECTION I: GENERAL OVERVIEW ===

Total Stations: 56
Number of Lines: 4
- East-West Line
- North-South Line
- Corridor-1
- Corridor-2

Operational Hours: 6:30 AM to 11:00 PM daily
Extended Hours: Up to 12:30 AM during major events at Narendra Modi Stadium

Operational Length (as of May 2025): 58.66 km
Phase 1: 40.03 km, 32 stations
Phase 2: 28.25 km, 22 stations (8 Gandhinagar stations under construction)

Fares:
- Minimum Fare: Rs 5
- Maximum Fare: Rs 40

Contact Information:
- Phone: +91-79-22960123
- Email: care@gujaratmetrorail.com

=== SECTION II: FARES AND TICKETING ===

Ticket Types: Single journey, Return journey, Group tickets (available via app or station counters)

Payments Accepted: Cash, UPI (Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay), Debit Cards, Credit Cards

Smart Cards:
- 10% fare discount on all journeys
- Purchase or recharge at stations or via the metro app
- Non-transferable between passengers
- Recharge range: Rs 50 to Rs 3000

NCMC (National Common Mobility Card):
- Accepted at all stations
- Foreign tourists: 3-month validity card available with valid passport and visa
- Recharge options: stations, app (UPI), bank ATMs, net banking

Refund Policy:
- Unused tickets/tokens are refundable within 30 minutes at point of purchase
- No refunds for used tickets or lost tokens/cards
- QR code tickets are refundable only if unused within 30 minutes of purchase

Penalties:
- Ticketless Travel: Rs 50 plus maximum fare
- Token Misuse: Rs 200

Children Policy: Up to 2 children under 3 feet tall can travel free per accompanying adult

=== SECTION III: STATION AMENITIES AND ACCESSIBILITY ===

Accessibility Features: All stations have elevators, escalators, and ramps for differently-abled passengers
Specific gate accessibility information available via "Station Info" section on app or website

Amenities at Stations:
- Restrooms available at all stations
- Free Wi-Fi available at select stations
- Mobile charging points: Not available at stations

Security Measures:
- CCTV surveillance at all stations and inside trains
- Baggage scanners at all station entry points

Parking:
- Parking available near most stations (not at all stations)
- Designated parking areas near Narendra Modi Stadium

=== SECTION IV: ROUTE AND TRAVEL GUIDELINES ===

Journey Planning: Use the "Plan Your Journey" feature on the metro app or website

Peak Hours on Weekdays:
- Morning Peak: 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM
- Evening Peak: 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM

Train Frequency: Varies by line and time (see Section V)

Line Interchanges (no additional fare charged):
- Old High Court station
- Motera Stadium station
- GNLU station

Luggage Policy:
- Maximum weight allowed: 25 kg
- Maximum dimensions: 80 x 50 x 30 cm
- Bicycles are prohibited on metro trains
- Mandatory security checks for all luggage

Prohibited Items on Metro:
- Flammable materials
- Explosives
- Toxic or radioactive materials
- Weapons of any kind
- Luggage exceeding size/weight limits
- Food and drinks (eating and drinking prohibited)
- Smoking is strictly prohibited
- Pets are not allowed

- Weekend Peak Hours: Every 12 minutes

North-South Line:

Corridor-1 (Gandhinagar):

Corridor-2 (GIFT City):

=== SECTION VII: LOST AND FOUND ===

How to Report Lost Items:
- Report at any metro station to station staff
- Contact Lost and Found Office directly

Lost and Found Office:
- Address: Apparel Park Depot, Rajpur Hirpur, Gomtipur, Ahmedabad - 380021
- Phone: 079-22960234

Lost Smart Cards: Report immediately at any station or to the Lost and Found Office
Items Lost on Train: Contact Metro Security or station staff immediately
Unclaimed Items: Kept for six months before disposal

=== SECTION VIII: EMERGENCY PROCEDURES ===

Medical Emergency:
- First aid kits available at all stations
- Ambulance: Call 1073
- Medical incidents must be reported within 24 hours to: claims@gujaratmetrorail.com

Fire Emergency:
- Use fire alarms immediately
- Follow emergency exit signage
- Do not use elevators during fire emergency

Lift/Elevator Issue:
- Press the emergency button inside the lift
- Or call: 079-22960000

Lost Child:
- Immediately inform station staff

Harassment or Safety Concerns:
- Press emergency button in coach or at station
- Women helpline: Call 181
- Police: Call 112

Security Concerns:
- Call: 079-22960234
- Use station intercoms

General Helpline: 079-22960000
