II. Fares and Ticketing
•	Ticket Types: Single, Return, Group (via app or counters)
•	Payments Accepted: Cash, UPI (Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay), Debit/Credit Cards
•	Smart Cards:
o	10% fare discount
o	Purchase/recharge at stations or via app
o	Non-transferable; Recharge range: ₹50 – ₹3000
•	NCMC Cards:
o	Accepted
o	Foreign tourists: 3-month validity card with passport/visa
o	Recharge via stations, app (UPI), bank ATMs, net banking
•	Refund Policy:
o	Unused tickets/tokens refundable within 30 minutes at point of purchase
o	No refunds for used tickets, lost tokens/cards
o	QR code tickets refundable only if unused within 30 minutes
•	Penalties:
o	Ticketless Travel: ₹50 + max fare
o	Token Misuse: ₹200
•	Children: Up to 2 under 3-feet-tall children travel free per adult
